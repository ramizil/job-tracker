Recruiter pitch — template

Short screen pitch (~45–60s). Edit in My Pitch — no personal details in this seed.

Three stops

Don't memorise words — memorise the three stops, then speak naturally.

  1. Who I am: Name, role/field, years of experience, and the kind of teams or systems you've worked with.
  2. What I do: Day-to-day strengths and 1–2 concrete examples (tools, impact).
  3. What I'm looking for: The role/environment you want next, and why you're a fit.

Hi — I'm [your name]. I work in [field] with about [N] years of experience… Recently I've focused on [strength / project]. I'm looking for [role type] where I can [contribution]. Happy to share more — what would be most useful?
